About Us

Mascom provides reliable mobile, internet, and digital services across Botswana with wide coverage and innovation.

Services

Mascom Wireless is one of Botswana's leading telecommunications providers, offering a wide range of services to meet both individual and business needs. These include mobile voice and data services, high-speed internet, mobile money solutions, and enterprise communication tools. With a commitment to innovation and customer satisfaction, Mascom continues to expand its network coverage and introduce cutting-edge technology, making connectivity more accessible and reliable across the country.
